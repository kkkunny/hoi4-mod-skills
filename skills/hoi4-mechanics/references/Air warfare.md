## Player view

- Air is managed per strategic region via the air map mode; planes need air bases (200 planes per level with no penalty, max level 10 = 2000; being over capacity costs −2% per 1% over, −100% at 50%; allies share capacity; captured bases auto-transfer their planes without loss). [w:Air warfare §空军基地 §容量]
- Land wings hold 100 planes, carrier wings 10; a wing must be a single plane type/mission; deployment takes 2 days, land↔carrier transfers disable reinforcement for 48 h, and disbanding returns planes to the stockpile. [w:Air warfare §航空队]
- Air superiority: each wing's power = planes × type value × mission efficiency; heavy fighters 1.25, fighters/CAS/NAV/TAC 1.00, strategic bombers 0.01; region colors: red <40%, contested 40–60%, green >60%; AA buildings subtract 5 power per level (max −25). [w:Air warfare §制空权]
- Superiority effects on land: −0.7% enemy defence/breakthrough per superiority point up to −35% (multiplied by doctrine bonuses), up to −30% enemy movement, and division AA can cut the penalty to 0.25× (non-linear); at sea it adds up to +25% detection; paradrops require ≥70% superiority. [w:Air warfare §制空权]
- Aces: one per wing; skills Good/Unique/Genius give fighter bonuses up to +10% attack, and ace effects scale inversely with wing size (up to ×50 at 2 planes); earn chance grows quadratically with wing size (≈2.98% for a full 100-plane wing); death chance 0.5% + 0.001% per plane lost. [w:Air warfare §王牌飞行员]
- Mission efficiency drives how many planes fly: icons red <50%, yellow <100%, green 100%; range gaps, base overcrowding, bad weather, lack of fuel and freshly assigned wings all reduce it; region efficiency is the weighted average of wings; more ground crews give +10% multiplicatively. [w:Air missions §Mission efficiency]
- Mission families: fighters/heavy fighters/jet fighters = air superiority (doubles as escort) or interception; CAS/TAC = CAS, strategic bombing, port strike, naval strike, logistics strike; naval bombers = naval strike/patrol/minelaying/minesweeping; strategic bombers = strategic bombing/minelaying; transports = air supply; scouts = recon/naval patrol. [w:Air missions §Mission efficiency]
- CAS: up to 3× the enemy battle width (terrain-adjusted) planes can join one battle; the friendly attack bonus is +25% base scaled by the participating fraction; CAS deals org/strength damage at 0.035 scaling; divisional AA cuts the damage by up to 75% (reached at 4.3 average AA attack) and has a 7% chance to shoot down up to 0.5% × air attack of the attackers. [w:Air missions §Close air support mission]
- Air-to-air combat runs every 8 hours: detection (occupation up to +10%, radar up to +50%, aircraft up to +80% at 3000 planes, night −20%, bad weather up to −90%) limits returns; at most 3× detected planes can attack; visibility differs by mission (CAS 2×, port strike 10×, escorted bombers fully visible). [w:Air combat §Air to air combat phase]
- Strategic bombing deals about 120 damage per cycle ≈0.3 buildings destroyed, distributed across the targeted building types; night gives a −50% chance of not finding the target; state AA (10% base chance, 0.8 damage factor) and technology modifiers reduce it. [w:Air missions §Strategic bombing mission] [w:Air combat §Ground Mission Phase]
- Air supply: each transport plane adds up to 0.01 supply to the region's state supply, +10% from more ground crews, at a running cost of 0.05 command points per plane. [w:Air missions §Air supply mission]
- Minelaying saturates a region at 1000 mines; without BBA the per-plane efficiency is 5% (naval bomber), 8% (TAC), 13% (strategic bomber); minesweeping is 10%/15%; with BBA the plane designer's Minelaying/Minesweeping stats replace these values. [w:Air missions §Naval minelaying mission §Naval minesweeping mission]
- Naval strike sorties have a base 4% chance to find an enemy fleet; targeting weights favour high-value ships (sub/screen ×10, capital ×50, carrier ×200, damaged up to +500%, low-AA multiplier); ships return fire with a 20% × max(0.9 − 0.02 × average plane agility, 0.01) chance, and fleet AA reduces strike damage by up to −75%. [w:Air missions §Naval strike mission] [w:Naval battle §海上打击]
