## Player view

- The navy is organised as fleets → task forces → ships; a task force sails at its slowest ship's speed; auto-reinforcement pulls from the theater's reserve fleet; repair options include auto-split-off, repair priority and "repair now". [w:Navy §特遣舰队 §舰队 §战区 §自动补充 §修理选项]
- The navy overview (hotkey P) lists fleet modifiers: escort efficiency 0%, sortie efficiency 50%, naval targeting 0%, port strikes 100%, carrier overcrowding 100%, convoy raiding 100%, naval invasion capacity 0. [w:Navy §修正项]
- Pride of the Fleet: +5% war support, −50% critical-hit chance for the ship, +25% XP gain and far fewer accidents; reassigning costs 100 PP; losing it costs −10% war support for 30 days. [w:Navy §舰队之傲]
- Battle layout: screen line, battle line (capitals), carrier group and submarine wolfpack; screening ratio = 3 screens per capital/carrier plus 0.5 per convoy = 100%, granting up to +40% hit chance and +20% retreat speed; carrier screening = 1 capital per carrier plus 0.25 per convoy, granting up to +10% sortie efficiency and +20% retreat speed. [w:Naval battle §Layout §屏护效率]
- Bad positioning scales the penalty up to −50% and can require up to double the screening ratios. [w:Naval battle §屏护效率]
- Weapons: heavy and light guns fire hourly, torpedoes usually every 4 hours (−25% with the Loading Drill Master trait); each ship targets up to three enemies per hour (one per weapon category: light, torpedo, heavy); target weights favour capitals and convoys (subs vs convoys 600×), reduced when damaged/retreating. [w:Naval battle §武器冷却 §目标选择]
- Submarines hide fully when undetected; a spotted sub has a cooldown before hiding again and is only attackable with depth charges; engagement rules decide whether subs attack screens or capitals. [w:Naval battle §潜艇]
- Hit chance: base 10% (depth charges 11%); ship hit profile = 100 × visibility / (0.5 × speed + 20), weapon profiles 45 (light gun) / 70 (heavy gun) / 100 (torpedo, depth charge); convoys 85; chance = base × modifiers × (profile ratio)², floored at 2%; low manpower −25% and low org −50% penalties. [w:Naval battle §命中率]
- Armour vs piercing ratio bands decide damage and critical multipliers (ratio ≥2 → full damage/2× crits; <0.1 → 0.1× damage/no crits); torpedoes ignore armour except heavy armour modules reduce their damage and crit chance. [w:Naval battle §装甲与穿透]
- Damage randomness is −2.5%…+47.5%; damage applies 60% to HP and 100% to org, with org damage immunity fading as HP drops; positioning penalises up to 50% and failing to penetrate can cut damage up to 90%. [w:Naval battle §Damage]
- Critical hits: torpedo crits 10% for ×2 damage; other weapons base 5% scaled inversely with reliability; parts table (magazine 10×, fires, propeller −90% speed, etc.). [w:Naval battle §「暴击」]
- Carrier mechanics: carriers can strike from hour 0 (screens only after 8 h, capitals after 6 h); base sortie efficiency 50%; deck overcrowding −2% per 1% over capacity; a carrier stacking penalty limits how many carrier air wings can target ships per side; only carrier-capable plane types may be based on them. [w:Naval battle §航母专属机制]
- Ship stats: attack/piercing pairs (light/heavy), torpedo, depth charge, armour, naval AA; speed, max range, org, deck size, HP, reliability, supply use, fuel use, surface/sub visibility, surface/sub detection; convoys are near-unarmed (0.25 light gun, 0.1 AA) and regain health between battles; detection is boosted by radar, air superiority and decryption advantage (+20%). [w:Ship §统计数值 §基础属性 §战斗属性 §其他属性 §运输船队]

## Unknowns

- Version conflicts (wiki quotes older patches; only local values are certain): `SUBMARINE_HIDE_TIMEOUT` wiki 20 vs local 8; `SUBMARINE_REVEALED_TIMEOUT` 16 vs 12; `SUBMARINE_REVEAL_ON_MINIMUM_POSITIONING` 2 vs 1.0; `COMBAT_MIN_HIT_CHANCE` 2% vs 0.005; `NAVAL_STRIKE_CARRIER_MULTIPLIER` page text ×10 vs local 12; carrier strike cadence text mixes 6 h/8 h while the define is 3.
- The "carrier stacking penalty" (CARRIER_STACK_PENALTY and CARRIER_STACK_PENALTY_EFFECT) is documented by the wiki but the define names are absent from the local 1.18.3 files; the mirror's worked example is garbled (verified by grep).
- The hidden-submarine passive detection formula is flagged by the wiki itself as probably erroneous (1.7.1-era code); treated as uncertain.
- Naval strike targeting weights are hardcoded in the executable (wiki note); only the define-level scales above are moddable.
- The floating-harbor cost line is unreadable in the mirror (icons lost) — not transcribed.
