## Player view

- States split into core, occupied (resistance active) and colonial (no resistance); resistance only continues while the state is non-core, non-impassable and another tag cores or claims it (plus legacy Spanish cases). [w:Occupation §导语]
- Resistance starts at 1% and moves toward a target; if it should not exist it decays −0.25%/day to 0; it is clamped to 0.01–100%. [w:Occupation §抵抗强度]
- Resistance thresholds: 25% organised resistance (+50% garrison penetration chance), 50% growing resistance (+100% garrison damage), 75% uprising (−50% division speed, no strategic redeployment, −50% local supply, +30% division attrition for the controller), 90% rebellion (−3% local factories; independence declared in the occupied core state). [w:Occupation §抵抗强度]
- Resistance target: base +35%, capitulated target +10%, exile government +2% plus up to +20% by legitimacy, claims −5%, peace −10%, VP tiers up to +20%, compliance up to −50%, controller stability up to +20% (`RESISTANCE_TARGET_MODIFIER_PER_STABILITY_LOSS = 0.2`), tiny-population discounts, plus modifiers (kind gentleman −5%, root-out mission −10%, laws −75%…+40%, spread from neighbours +0.5% per step); the target floors at 10% until compliance pushes it to 0, and resistance only re-enables when the target reaches 10% again. [w:Occupation §抵抗度目标]
- Resistance growth/decay: +0.2%/day growth, −0.1%/day decay (multiplied by modifiers); claim gives +25% decay; below 10%/20% resistance decay shifts −50%/−25%. [w:Occupation §抵抗的增长与衰减]
- Resistance activity: chance = +0.312%/point of resistance (max +31.2% at 100%); partisans destroy 2% of garrison equipment and 1.6% of garrison manpower per activity; garrison hardness reduces both by 1% per point up to −90%, and total damage reduction caps at −90%; uprisings can break infrastructure/factories at a base 2% per activity. [w:Occupation §抵抗活动]
- Compliance: starts 0% (70% historical for starting non-cores; 50% of the previous controller's value on transfer), grows 0.075%/day base (+claims +5, peace +10, laws −0.11…+0.03/day), decays with a −0.083%/day cap and exile legitimacy up to −0.015%/day; historical non-core states start at 70%. [w:Occupation §顺从度]
- Compliance converts to industry: factories 25% + 65% × compliance; resources 35% + 65% × compliance; manpower 2% + 18% × compliance (100% compliance ⇒ 100% factories, 105% resources, 40.5% manpower); thresholds at 15/25/40/60/80% compliance unlock informants, local police (−25% garrison need), reorganized workforce (+10% local resources/factories), volunteer program (+20% recruitable), new regime (collaboration government event). [w:Occupation §顺从度与工业]
- Garrisons: garrison divisions are hidden motorized templates auto-equipped; law effects scale with garrison efficiency `100% − max(manpower_req², equipment_req³)`; each resistance point needs 0.75 suppression, with the need counted as at least 10% resistance and capped at 50%. [w:Occupation §驻军]
- Occupation laws (generic): No Garrison (+40% target, no compliance), Civilian Oversight (−25% garrison need/damage), Local Police (−15%/−35%/−50%, −0.025% compliance), Secret Police (−30%/−15%/−15%, +5% resources/factories), Military Governor (−35%, +10% resources, +8% manpower), Forced Labour (−40%, +40% resources, +25% repair), Harsh Quotas (−40%, +5% resources/+25% factories), Martial Law (−60%, +10% resources), Brutal Oppression (−75%, fascist only), Liberated Workers (communist only), Local Autonomy (+10% target, democratic only). [w:Occupation §占领法令]
- Collaboration governments: built via the collaboration operation to raise cooperation (each use +30–45% starting compliance, cap 100%); at 80% average cooperation a collaboration government can be installed (not for democracies), which removes resistance and is a special puppet that a player can later upgrade to independence. [w:Occupation §合作政府]

## Unknowns

- The page's stability→resistance sentence mixes a −0.2%/point wording with a "+20% max"; the define is a positive 0.2 factor; the page wording is kept only as a note.
- Country-specific occupation laws (Turkey, Italy, British Raj, Japan, Belgium) are content; only the generic laws are transcribed.
- The "garrison command base limit" figures quoted in planning documents (24/72) come from a different page (`Land warfare command.md`) and were flagged uncertain there.
- Collaboration-government mechanics overlap with `Puppet.md`; only occupation-side numbers are kept here.
